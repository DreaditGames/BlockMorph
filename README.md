# BlockMorph

A Fabric Quality of Life (QoL) mod designed to eliminate inventory clutter while building.

Instead of filling your inventory with stairs, slabs, fences, and signs, BlockMorph allows you to carry just your base blocks and freely swap between variants on the fly.

### Features
* **Morphing:** Quickly swap a base block into its variants (stairs, slabs, etc.) without using a crafting table.
* **Vanilla-Friendly Ratios:** The mod respects standard vanilla crafting ratios, with a few extra QoL tweaks to make building smoother (e.g., signs require one less stick, hanging signs do not require chains and you can finally get a 1 for 1 with wooden stairs!).
* **Collapse Leftovers:** Finished building? Hold your leftover variant blocks in your hand and **double-click the `V` key (Hold shift for stacks)**. As long as you have the correct ratio of items, they will instantly collapse back into their base block form, keeping your inventory completely clean.

### Installation
This is a Fabric mod. Simply drop the `.jar` file into your `mods` folder.
